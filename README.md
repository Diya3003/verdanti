# verdanti.ai

*Smarter landscapes. Less water.*

**Try it:** https://verdantiai.streamlit.app

verdanti.ai helps homeowners and HOAs in Santa Clara County replace their lawn with a
drought-tolerant garden. Upload a photo of your lawn and get, all in one place:

- A garden design and an image of it in your own yard
- A plant list using plants that qualify for Valley Water's lawn rebate
- Your estimated rebate and water savings
- Local rebate programs and next steps
- A link to find trained local landscapers

This is the actively updated version of the app, built with feedback from local landscapers.
The original version submitted to the 2026 Congressional App Challenge is at
[Diya3003/verdanti.ai](https://github.com/Diya3003/verdanti.ai) and is not being changed.

Estimates only. Valley Water's pre-inspection makes the final rebate decision.
